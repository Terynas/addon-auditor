# PKG003: Missing LICENSE or CHANGELOG

**Category:** packaging

## Why

Without a license nobody knows whether they may fork, fix or redistribute the addon. Without a changelog users cannot tell what an update changed (CurseForge/Wago show `manual-changelog`).

## Bad

```text
MyAddon/
  MyAddon.toc
  core.lua
```

## Good

```text
MyAddon/
  MyAddon.toc
  core.lua
  LICENSE
  CHANGELOG.md
```
